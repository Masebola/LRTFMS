Appeal Guidelines

  • Appeals must be submitted within 32 days of fine issuance
  • You will receive a response within 14 business days
  • The fine remains payable until the appeal is approved
  • False declarations may result in additional penalties

Important Information

Before You Appeal:

  • Ensure you have the correct Fine ID
  • Gather any supporting documents
  • Be prepared to provide detailed information
  • False declarations are punishable by law

Valid Appeal Reasons:

  • Incorrect vehicle or driver details
  • You were not driving the vehicle
  • Vehicle was stolen at the time
  • Faulty speed camera or equipment
  • Emergency circumstances
